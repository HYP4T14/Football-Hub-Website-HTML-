Persib Bandung

History

Persib Bandung traces its origins to Bandoeng Inlandsche Voetbal Bond (BIVB), which was established on 5 January 1919. BIVB became one of the seven organizations involved in the founding of PSSI in 1930 and later transformed into Persib after merging with another Bandung football organization in 1934.

Persib subsequently developed into one of Indonesia's major football clubs and became a professional organization under PT Persib Bandung Bermartabat in 2009. The club has continued competing at the highest level and entered the 2026/27 season as the defending Super League champion.

Achievements

  • Liga Indonesia Champion - 1994/95
  • Indonesia Super League Champion - 2014
  • Liga 1 Champion - 2023/24
  • Super League Champion - 2025/26
