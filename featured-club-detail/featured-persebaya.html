Persebaya Surabaya

History

Persebaya Surabaya was founded on 8 June 1927 as Soerabhaisasche Indonesische Voetbal Bond (SIVB). The club later became one of the founding organizations behind the establishment of PSSI in 1930, making Persebaya one of the historically significant clubs in Indonesian football.

Throughout its history, Persebaya has competed in several eras of Indonesian football. The club has won championships during both the Perserikatan era and the modern Liga Indonesia era, while also producing numerous players who went on to represent Indonesia internationally.

Achievements

  • Perserikatan Champion - 1941, 1950, 1951, 1952, 1975/78, 1987/88
  • Liga Indonesia Champion - 1996, 2004
