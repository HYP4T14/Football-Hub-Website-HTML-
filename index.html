Introduction

Welcome to Indonesia League One! A website dedicated to exploring the world of Indonesian professional football.

Indonesia League One presents information about the competition, participating clubs, legendary moments, players, standings, and the passionate football culture that surrounds the league. From intense rivalries to unforgettable matches, the league reflects the enthusiasm of millions of football supporters across Indonesia.

Through this website, you can learn more about the teams competing in Indonesia's top level of domestic football, discover important information about the competition, and explore the stories that make Indonesian football unique.

Feel the passion. Know the clubs. Experience Indonesia's football.

History

  1. The Fondation - Perserikatan & Galatama

    2. Before Liga 1 existed, Indonesian football had already developed a long tradition of national-level competitions.

    Two important competitions shaped the early history of Indonesian top-flight football, Perserikatan and Galatama.

    Perserikatan was closely associated with football associations representing cities or regions. Meanwhile, Galatama introduced a different model in which privately managed clubs competed in a professional-oriented competition.

    Galatama also produced clubs that later became important parts of Indonesian football history. Arema FC, for example, was founded in 1987 and was one of the clubs associated with the Galatama era. The club's official league profile records its Galatama championship in the 1992–93 season.

  2. 1994 - The Formation of Liga Indonesia

    3. A major change occurred in 1994, when Perserikatan and Galatama were brought together into a unified national competition known as Liga Indonesia.

    This merger was significant because it brought together traditions from the regional-association system and the professional-club system into one national competition.

    The 1994 transition therefore represents an important point in the development of Indonesian domestic football.

  3. The Liga Indonesia Era

    4. During the Liga Indonesia era, clubs from different regions of the country competed for the national championship.

    The competition became an important platform for Indonesian clubs and players, while traditional regional rivalries continued to play a major role in football culture.

    Several clubs that remain significant today have roots extending back to this period. Their histories demonstrate that modern Liga 1 did not appear independently; it developed from decades of domestic football competition.

  4. 2008 - The Indonesia Super League Era

    5. Indonesian top-flight football subsequently entered the Indonesia Super League (ISL) era.

    The 2008–09 season is an important point in the development of the competition. The current league's historical documentation describes the top division at that time as the Liga Super Indonesia, preceding the later Liga 1 era.

    The ISL period featured many established Indonesian clubs and became an important chapter in the development of professional domestic football.

    One example is Arema, which won the Indonesia Super League in the 2009-10 season.

  5. 2017 - The Birth of Liga 1

    6. The next major milestone came in 2017, when the competition adopted the name Liga 1.

    This is the most important date for your website because it marks the beginning of the modern Liga 1 era.

    The inaugural Liga 1 season was known as Go-Jek Traveloka Liga 1 2017. Bhayangkara FC finished as the champion of the 2017 competition. The official league profile for Bhayangkara records its 2017 championship.

    This created a new chapter in Indonesian professional football, with Liga 1 becoming the recognizable name of the country's top-level domestic competition.
