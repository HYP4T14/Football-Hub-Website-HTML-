Persija Jakarta

History

Persija Jakarta originated as Voetbalbond Indonesia Jacatra (VIJ), founded on 28 November 1928. During the colonial period, VIJ represented Indonesian nationalist football organizations in Batavia and subsequently became one of the organizations that helped establish PSSI on 19 April 1930.

VIJ officially transformed into Persija in 1950. Since then, the club has developed into one of Indonesia's most historically successful football institutions and is closely associated with Jakarta and its supporter community, The Jakmania.

Achievements

  • National Champion - 1931, 1933, 1934, 1938, 1954, 1964, 1973, 1975, 1979, 2001, 2018
