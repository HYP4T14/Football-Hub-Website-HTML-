Persik Kediri

History

Persik Kediri was founded in 1950 and represents Kediri, East Java. The club is known as Macan Putih, while its supporters are commonly known as Persik Mania. The club's purple identity has become strongly associated with its history and supporters.

Persik became particularly successful during the early 2000s, winning Indonesia's top-level championship twice. After experiencing periods outside the top division, the club rebuilt itself by winning Liga 3 in 2018 and Liga 2 in 2019 before returning to Liga 1.

Achievements

  • Liga Indonesia Champion - 2003
  • Liga Indonesia Champion - 2006
  • Liga 3 Champion - 2018
  • Liga 2 Champion - 2019
