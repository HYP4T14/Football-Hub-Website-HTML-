Malut United FC

History

Malut United was established in 2023 and represents Maluku Utara. The club entered the national football pyramid in the 2023/24 Liga 2 season and immediately achieved promotion to the top division, making its rise one of the fastest among Indonesia's newer professional clubs.

After reaching the top division, Malut United continued to make an impact and finished among the top three in the 2024/25 Liga 1 season. For the 2026/27 competition, the club is presented under the Java United FC identity, while its historical record originates from Malut United.

Achievements

  • Liga 2 promotion - 2023/24
  • Top-three Liga 1 finish - 2024/25
  • Established top-flight club
