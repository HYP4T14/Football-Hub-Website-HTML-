Persita Tangerang

History

Persita Tangerang is based in Tangerang, Banten, and has a history dating back to the early development of organized football in the region. The club is traditionally known by the nickname Pendekar Cisadane and has established itself as one of the recognized clubs from Banten.

Persita has competed in different levels of Indonesian football and reached the top division multiple times. The club returned to the highest level after finishing as runner-up in Liga 2 in 2019 and has remained part of the modern Indonesian top-flight system.

Achievements

  • Divisi I Champion - 1993/94
  • Liga Indonesia Runner-up - 2002
  • Liga 2 Runner-up - 2019
  • Promotion to Liga 1 - 2020
