PSS Sleman

History

PSS Sleman was founded on 20 May 1976 in Sleman, Yogyakarta. The club began its competitive journey in the lower divisions of Indonesian football and gradually progressed through the national football pyramid. In 1996, PSS won the Division II competition in the Yogyakarta region.

PSS later progressed through the national divisions and eventually reached Indonesia's top division in the 2018/19 season. After relegation in 2024/25, the club returned to the highest level for the 2026/27 season, with its identity closely connected to Slemania and Brigata Curva Sud.

Achievements

  • Divisi II DIY Champion - 1996
  • Regional Jateng Champion
  • Promotion to Liga 1 - 2018/19
  • Return to the top division - 2026/27
