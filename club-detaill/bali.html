Bali United FC

History

Bali United FC became the continuation of the football organization previously associated with Persisam Putra Samarinda after its relocation to Bali. The club was established under its current identity in 2015 and quickly became an important club in Indonesian top-flight football.

Based in Bali and known as Serdadu Tridatu, Bali United developed into a competitive modern club and regularly competed at the highest level of Indonesian football. Its growth was accompanied by domestic success and appearances in Asian competitions.

Achievements

  • Liga 1 Champion - 2019
  • Liga 1 Champion - 2021/22
  • Regular participation in Indonesian top-flight football
  • Asian competition appearances
