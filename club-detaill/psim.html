PSIM Yogyakarta

History

PSIM Yogyakarta was founded on 5 September 1929 under the name Perserikatan Sepak Raga Mataram. The club became one of the pioneers of Indonesian football and was involved in the establishment of PSSI in 1930. Its historical identity is strongly connected to Yogyakarta and the cultural heritage of Mataram.

PSIM has competed through different periods of Indonesian football and won the Perserikatan championship in 1932. The club eventually returned to the country's top division for the 2026/27 season, bringing its historic identity back to the highest level of Indonesian football.

Achievements

  • Perserikatan Champion - 1932
  • Several Perserikatan runner-up finishes
  • Promotion to Super League - 2026/27
