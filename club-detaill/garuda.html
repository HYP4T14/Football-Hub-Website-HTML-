Garudayaksa FC

History

Garudayaksa FC is one of the newest clubs in Indonesian professional football, having been founded in 2025. The club made its professional debut in the 2025/26 Pegadaian Championship, the second tier of Indonesian football.

The club's stated objective is to establish a professionally managed organization capable of competing at national level while also developing talented footballers. After its debut season, Garudayaksa earned promotion to the 2026/27 Super League.

Achievements

  • Pegadaian Championship Champion - 2025/26
  • Promotion to Super League - 2026/27
  • First Super League season - 2026/27
