Persijap Jepara

History

Persijap Jepara is a football club from Jepara, Central Java, with a long history in Indonesian national competitions. The club has spent periods competing in the top division and lower tiers throughout its history and is traditionally associated with the nickname Laskar Kalinyamat.

After spending time outside the highest level of Indonesian football, Persijap returned to the Super League for the 2026/27 season. The club entered the campaign with a mixture of experienced domestic players and international recruits as it sought to establish itself in the top division again.
