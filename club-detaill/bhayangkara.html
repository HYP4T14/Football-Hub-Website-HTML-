Bhayangkara FC

History

Bhayangkara FC began its professional football journey in 2016, with its first official Liga 1 campaign taking place in 2017. The club quickly established itself in the Indonesian top division and adopted the nickname The Guardian.

The club subsequently became a regular participant in Indonesia's highest division. In the 2026/27 season, the club competes under the name Bhayangkara Presisi Lampung FC, with its home matches based at PKOR Sumpah Pemuda in Bandar Lampung.

Achievements

  • Liga 1 Champion - 2017
  • Third place - 2018
  • Third place - 2021/22
  • Regular top-flight participation
