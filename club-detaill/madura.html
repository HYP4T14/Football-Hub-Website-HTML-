Madura United FC

History

Madura United FC was established in 2016 and represents the island of Madura in Indonesian professional football. The club inherited the football structure associated with its predecessor and developed a distinct identity as one of the established clubs from East Java.

Since entering the top division, Madura United has remained a regular participant in Indonesia's highest level of football. The club is known by the nickname Sape Kerrab and plays its home matches at Gelora Madura Ratu Pamelingan Stadium.

Achievements

  • Regular top-flight participation since 2016
  • Several appearances in the upper half of the Indonesian league
  • Established itself as one of the leading clubs from Madura
