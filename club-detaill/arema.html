Arema FC

History

Arema FC was officially founded on 11 August 1987 and became one of the clubs associated with the Galatama era of Indonesian football. Based in Malang, the club is widely known by its nickname Singo Edan and has developed a strong football identity connected with the Malang region.

Throughout its history, Arema has competed in different eras of Indonesian football and has also represented Indonesia in Asian competitions. The club has maintained its status as one of the established professional clubs in the country, with its history spanning the Galatama era, the Indonesia Super League era and the modern Super League era.

Achievements

  • Galatama Champion - 1992/93
  • Indonesia Super League Champion - 2009/10
  • Piala Indonesia Champion - 2005, 2006
  • Piala Presiden Champion - 2017, 2019, 2022
  • Asian competition appearances
