Borneo FC Samarinda

History

Borneo FC Samarinda was founded on 7 March 2014 and is based in Samarinda, East Kalimantan. The club was originally known as Pusamania Borneo FC and adopted the Borneo FC Samarinda identity as it developed into a major professional club.

Despite being a relatively young club, Borneo FC achieved success very quickly. Within its first year, the club won the Indonesian second-tier championship in 2014 and subsequently established itself as a regular participant in the top division.

Achievements

  • Divisi Utama Champion - 2014
  • Established top-flight club
  • Regular participation in national competitions
