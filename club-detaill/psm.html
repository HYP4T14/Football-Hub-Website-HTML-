PSM Makassar

History

PSM Makassar is one of the historic football clubs representing South Sulawesi and the city of Makassar. The club has been an important participant in Indonesian football for decades and has developed a strong identity as Juku Eja.

PSM has competed across several eras of Indonesian football and has regularly participated in the country's highest division. In the modern era, the club returned to the top of Indonesian football by winning Liga 1 in the 2022/23 season.

Achievements

  • Multiple national championship achievements
  • Liga 1 Champion - 2022/23
  • Regular top-flight participation
  • Asian competition appearances
