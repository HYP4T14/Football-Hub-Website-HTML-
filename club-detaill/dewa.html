Dewa United Banten FC

History

Dewa United was established in 2021 and began its rise through the Indonesian football pyramid in the second tier. The club progressed rapidly and reached the top division, where it established itself as a relatively young but ambitious professional club.

The club is now based in Banten and plays its home matches at Banten International Stadium. Its rapid development has included the recruitment of experienced Indonesian and international players, helping Dewa United become a competitive Super League side.

Achievements

  • Promotion to the Indonesian top flight
  • Established Super League status
  • Participation in Asian competition
  • Strong domestic finishes in recent seasons
